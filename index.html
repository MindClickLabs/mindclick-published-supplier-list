How to read a rating

MSAP ratings have three levels. A supplier’s rating covers its rated products in that category, so it can be a single level or a range.

StarterEntry levelAchieverMiddle levelLeaderHighest level
Leader: every rated product in the category is at Leader.
Achiever–Leader: rated products range from Achiever to Leader. MindClick confirms each product in step 3.
In process: the assessment is under way and there’s no rating yet.
